## Acceptance criteria (all must pass)

### A. Structured, instanced racks (fixes "black slab with confetti" problem)
1. Rack = group of instanced meshes: body boxes, per-unit **bezel faces**
   (thin lighter strips so units read as separate servers), and **aligned
   LED clusters**: each unit gets a fixed row of 3–5 tiny LEDs at the SAME
   x-offsets per unit (structure!), colors chosen per-unit:
   healthy rack ≈ 70% green / 20% off / 10% amber; "hot" rack ≈ 40% green /
   35% amber / 15% red / 10% off.
2. Use `THREE.InstancedMesh` for bodies, bezels, and LEDs separately.
   Target scene totals: ~14–18 racks per side over walk length ~46 m,
   ~40–60 draw calls TOTAL (report `renderer.info.render.calls` in HUD).
3. Bezel material must catch light (metalness ~0.6, roughness ~0.35) so
   rack faces are visible, not pure black. Slight per-rack emissive tint
   strip (very dim teal, emissiveIntensity ~0.25) at the top of each rack
   (service light) to break silhouettes.
4. LED twinkle: each frame update ~1% of LED instance colors via
   `instanceColor` buffer (blink/swap), `instanceColor.needsUpdate = true`.

### B. Scroll = walk
1. No page scrollbar. Virtual scroll: `wheel` deltas, touch vertical drag,
   and ArrowUp/Down/PageUp/PageDown/Space all feed one `target` (0..1).
   Smooth-follow: `current += (target - current) * min(1, dt*3)`.
2. Camera path: straight dolly down the aisle center, eye height 1.6,
   z from +11 (entry) to -35 (near core beacon). current=0 → z=+11,
   current=1 → z=-35. Mouse x/y adds small parallax look offset
   (±0.25 rad max, eased). On touch devices skip parallax.
3. HUD hint at start: `~ scroll or drag to walk the aisle ~` fades out
   after first scroll input. HUD keeps fps/draw-calls line (qa evidence).
4. prefers-reduced-motion: disable parallax + twinkle; scroll jumps
   camera directly (no easing).

### C. Real lighting upgrade
1. Replace my canvas-gradient environment with
   `three/addons/environments/RoomEnvironment.js` + PMREMGenerator
   (import from 'three/addons/...'). Keep scene dark: ambient contribution
   stays low (`scene.environmentIntensity` or material envMapIntensity ~0.5).
2. Bloom: `EffectComposer` + `RenderPass` + `UnrealBloomPass`
   (strength 0.55, radius 0.4, threshold 0.85 — only LEDs + the core
   beacon bloom, not the whole frame) + `OutputPass` LAST. Renderer stays
   ACESFilmic. Resize must resize composer too.
3. Keep: FogExp2 (maybe loosen to 0.045 so the walk reveals depth
   gradually), floor seams, ceiling trays, the warm accent beacon at the
   far end (position z=-26 → move to z=-45; walk ends before it — it stays
   the "someday" light. Its sphere gets a slow pulse: scale 1±0.04 sin t).
